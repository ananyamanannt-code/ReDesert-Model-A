Scientific interpretation

This is a satellite-defined vegetation-greenness proxy, not proof of native plant survival, available groundwater, soil fertility, or irrigation-free planting. Predictions should not be used as planting prescriptions.

What the test tells us

The full eight-feature Random Forest tied the NDVI-only model on test accuracy; an NDVI threshold model performed similarly. More field observations and evaluation across other Dubai sites are needed.

Data: NASA HLS and supporting environmental datasets, collected through Google Earth Engine; 360 balanced proxy-labelled samples from Lahbab, Dubai. Models evaluated on disjoint ~1.5 km blocks. Interactive file is a research demonstration.
